# Vossie Marketplace: 15:00 launch film, working plan

One pre-produced 15:00 film, one AI narrator (calm male, keynote style), 16:9, 4K master plus a 1080p copy. Vossie blue `#396573` on black, charcoal and white. The app is the star: every product shot is the real app, recorded slowly (about 3 to 4 seconds per screen) inside a silver 3D phone. No invented statistics. No competitor price comparison.

Tagline: **One campus. Multiple opportunities. One connected marketplace.** End card: **DISCOVER. CONNECT. TRADE.**

## Study notes: what the film says

Notes appear twice, inside the buyer scene and the seller scene, as part of the same story.

**Buyer scene, about 25 seconds.** Open Books. A banner says Study notes by module. Search a module by name or code, pick your campus. Open a pack: cover, sample page and contents, the module, code, campus, lecturer and page count, reviews from other students. Read the first two pages free. Pay once, and the PDF opens straight away. It stays in My study notes.

Narration:

> "Studying for a test? Open Books, then Study notes. Search any module by its name or code, and pick your campus. Read the first two pages free. Pay once, and your notes open straight away. No delivery, no waiting. And they stay in your library."

**Seller scene, about 20 seconds.** A student who passed a module turns their notes into income. In Add product, choose Books and switch on Digital study notes. Enter the module name and code, pick the campus, upload the PDF, set a price and publish. The pack appears next to every other listing.

Narration:

> "Passed a module? Turn your notes into income. Add the module name and code, choose your campus, upload your PDF and publish. Your pack sits next to every other listing, and buyers get it the moment they pay."

On screen for both: one line, **Study notes by module and campus.** Nothing about other companies or prices elsewhere.
