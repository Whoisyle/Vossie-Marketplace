# Vossie launch film: narration script

One narrator, calm male keynote voice, about 2.2 words per second with pauses. Lines are timed to the scene windows in `KEYNOTE-PLAN.md`. `[pause]` is a beat of silence, `[...]` is a gap for the picture to speak. No statistics, no revenue or adoption claims, nothing about other companies.

Word budget per scene is shown so the voice never runs over its window.

---

## 01 Opening, 00:00 to 01:15 (budget 110 words)

[Black, 6 seconds. Campus. Hands and phones.]

> Every campus has an economy. [pause]
> People looking for something. Students building something. Products changing hands.
> [pause]
> But what happens when all of it is connected? [pause]
> Introducing Vossie Marketplace.

## 02 Welcome, 01:15 to 02:30 (budget 120 words)

> Welcome, and thank you for being here.
> Every day, students on campus buy, sell and discover. They find things in group chats. They sell through conversations. They arrange hand-overs by message.
> It works, but it is scattered.
> We think those moments belong together. [pause]
> That is Vossie.

On screen: BUY. SELL. DISCOVER. into the logo.

## 03 The opportunity, 02:30 to 04:00 (budget 160 words)

> Picture an ordinary day.
> A student needs something, so they search, ask around and wait. [pause]
> A student entrepreneur has something to sell, and has to find customers, answer questions and arrange hand-over.
> And once someone buys, there is one more question: how does it get from one person to the other?
> These are one story, told in three disconnected places. [pause]
> Vossie connects them.

On screen: DISCOVERY. COMMERCE. FULFILMENT. joined by lines.

## 04 The ecosystem, 04:00 to 05:30 (budget 160 words)

> At the centre of Vossie is a simple idea. [pause]
> Different people share one marketplace, but each needs different tools.
> A buyer needs to find things and keep track of them. A seller needs to show what they have and run their shop. A courier needs to take a job and get it done.
> One app. Three roles. One connected marketplace. [pause]
> Let me show you.

On screen: BUYER, SELLER, COURIER around the marketplace, into the phone.

## 05 The buyer, 05:30 to 07:00 (budget 200 words)

> We start where every student starts.
> The intro. Then sign in with your Eduvos account, and your student card is verified. [pause]
> Home brings together what is popular on your campus. Explore helps you find anything, by category or by search. Reels shows products the way people actually browse. Activity keeps your orders in one place, and Account holds everything personal.
> [pause]
> Studying for a test? Open Books, then Study notes. Search any module by its name or code, and pick your campus. Read the first two pages free. Pay once, and your notes open straight away. No delivery, no waiting. And they stay in your library. [pause]
> Ordering something physical works the same way: pick it, pay, and follow it to your hands.

Notes shot list (about 25 s): Books chip, Study notes banner, browse and campus filter, listing (cover, sample page, contents), reviews, free preview, locked page, pay sheet, reader, My study notes.

## 06 The seller, 07:00 to 08:45 (budget 210 words)

> Now the other side of the marketplace.
> Anyone can become a seller. You choose what kind of seller you are, add your documents, and Vossie Operations checks them. Then you set up your shop: your name, your logo, your banner, and where to find you. [pause]
> Adding a product takes a minute. A photo, a name, a price. [pause]
> Passed a module? Turn your notes into income. Choose Books, switch on digital study notes, add the module name and code, pick your campus, upload your PDF and publish. Your pack sits next to every other listing, and buyers get it the moment they pay. [pause]
> When an order arrives you accept it, prepare it, and hand it to a courier with a code. Your earnings and payouts are always in front of you.

## 07 Courier and Campus Hub, 08:45 to 10:15 (budget 160 words)

> A marketplace does not end at checkout. It ends when the item arrives. [pause]
> Couriers sign up, verify, and choose when they work. A job arrives, they collect it with the seller's code, and deliver it with the buyer's code. The buyer can follow along, message or call. [pause]
> Behind that sits Campus Hub: a place on campus where items can wait, be handed over and be collected. [pause]
> Seller, hub, courier, buyer. One clear journey.

On screen: SELLER, CAMPUS HUB, COURIER, BUYER.

## 08 Vossie Operations, 10:15 to 11:15 (budget 120 words)

> Behind every good marketplace is a team keeping it running.
> Vossie Operations gives that team four views. [pause]
> Campus Hub, to see what is waiting and hand it over. Transfers, to follow every item from seller to buyer. Incubation, to support the student businesses we want to grow. And an activity log, so every approval and every hand-over can be traced.

## 09 Trust and sign-in, 11:15 to 12:30 (budget 140 words)

> Technology is not only about what a product can do. It is about how it feels to use. [pause]
> Vossie is built around its community. You sign in with your Eduvos account. Your role decides what you see. Everything follows the same clear design, so a new buyer, a new seller and a new courier all feel at home.
> [pause]
> The best technology is not just powerful. It is easy to use.

## 10 The opportunity, 12:30 to 13:45 (budget 150 words)

> What makes Vossie interesting is what sits around a single sale. [pause]
> Buyers, sellers, delivery and campus operations, working as one. [pause]
> For students, a place made for campus life. For student entrepreneurs, a way to reach customers. For campuses and partners, a more connected way to bring commerce onto campus.
> And for investors, an integrated marketplace that can grow beyond where it starts.
> Our focus now is simple: prove the experience, earn real participation, and build the foundations to grow responsibly.

No numbers on screen. Segments: Users. Sellers. Transactions. Operations.

## 11 Roadmap, 13:45 to 14:30 (budget 100 words)

> Today is an introduction, and it is also a starting point. [pause]
> Next: Build, by refining the product. Validate, by testing with real students. Scale, by growing carefully, campus by campus.
> Everything on this roadmap is proposed. We will earn each step. [pause]
> That is what we are building with Vossie.

On screen: BUILD. VALIDATE. SCALE. Each labelled "proposed".

## 12 Close, 14:30 to 15:00 (budget 55 words)

> One campus. Multiple opportunities. One connected marketplace. [pause]
> This is Vossie Marketplace. [pause]
> Thank you.

End card: **DISCOVER. CONNECT. TRADE.** Hold.

---

## Voice notes

- Calm, warm, measured; slow down on the lines before each `[pause]`.
- Say "Vossie" as in "Voss-ee". Say "Eduvos" as in "Ed-oo-vos".
- Nothing here claims revenue, users, partners or institutional approval. If you are asked about the Eduvos sign-in on the day, answer that it is a prototype of the sign-in and that no institutional approval is being claimed.
