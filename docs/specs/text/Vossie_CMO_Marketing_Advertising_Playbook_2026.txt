recurring diagonal powder-blue panel is the campaign device.

16. Outdoor compliance & placement control
Do not print or install street-pole material until the specific municipality, road authority, landowner and campus requirements are
checked.
South Africa's SAMOAC framework explicitly covers lamppost advertising, street furniture, large posters and commercial signs at
educational facilities. Municipal rules vary. Johannesburg's Outdoor Advertising By-law regulates signs visible from public places/roads
and includes approval and safety restrictions. Therefore the campaign concept can be standardized, but dimensions, mounting, duration
and permissions must be localized.

19. CMO launch recommendation
Build recognition first, then liquidity, then repeat behaviour.
The launch should open with the master campaign “GOOD THINGS. BRIGHTER CAMPUS.” and the recognition question “WHAT DO
YOU NEED?”. Street/campus posters and digital creative create the memory structure; Market Day and ambassadors convert it into
verified users and supply; Requests expose missing demand; Reels make student sellers discoverable; courier content follows only
once the fulfilment flow is stable.
The key strategic discipline is to avoid advertising Vossie as if every feature is already production-live. Campaign claims must follow the
actual pilot state. This keeps the brand principle of dependability intact and protects trust during the highest-risk phase: first use.
Source notes: Brand identity and logo rules are taken from the existing Vossie Brand & UI/UX Profile. Market-channel context uses current South African
social-platform data and student-marketing channel evidence. Outdoor concepts must be localized against municipal/campus approvals and the SAMOAC
framework.
