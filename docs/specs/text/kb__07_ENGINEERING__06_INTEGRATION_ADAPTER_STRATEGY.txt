Integration Adapter Strategy
All external dependencies should sit behind V ossie-owned interfaces/adapters.
Identity
Prototype simulated institutional sign-in; production Microsoft/Entra/Eduvos federation if approved.
Payments
Sandbox provider adapter now; production PSP later.
Maps/location
Provider-neutral map/location layer where possible.
Third-party courier
Provider adapter converts external tracking/events into V ossie shipment states.
Notifications
In-app is internal; push/email providers remain replaceable.
Storage/media
Prototype may use Supabase Storage; video-heavy Reels may later move to a more suitable media/CDN service.
Why
Adapters prevent the prototype from becoming permanently coupled to whichever free service was easiest during the Hack
Jam.
