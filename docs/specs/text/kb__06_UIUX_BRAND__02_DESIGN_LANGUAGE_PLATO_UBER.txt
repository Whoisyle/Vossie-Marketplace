Design Language: Plato-Inspired Visuals, Uber-Inspired F unction
Plato-inspired visual literacy
Use as inspiration, not as copied proprietary design:
• airy layouts,
• gentle colour panels,
• warm surfaces,
• restrained typography ,
• rich product photography ,
• minimal visual noise,
• soft premium feeling.
Uber-inspired interaction literacy
Again, use patterns not proprietary copying:
• clear destinations,
• contextual cards,
• strong primary actions,
• bottom navigation on mobile,
• sheets/drawers,
• live status,
• map-centric courier flow,
• clear task switching,
• concise activity history ,
• progressive disclosure.
V ossie rule
Visuals must remain unmistakably V ossie. Brand Identity wins over references.
Performance/latency feeling
Microinteractions should feel immediate. Essential actions must never wait for decorative animation. Stable typography
and predictable state changes are more important than flashy transitions.
