Activation
Courier is a capability on the same verified student identity .
T ransport modes:
• walking,
• bicycle,
• motorcycle,
• car.
Eligibility/matching considers distance, package size/weight, route, time and transport mode.
Courier work loop
Go Online → eligible job card → map, distance, package class, payout → accept/decline → navigate to pickup → verify/scan
custody → route to buyer/hub → OTP/scan handover → complete → earning state updates.
V erification
W alking: low-friction radius/availability policy .
Bicycle: transport declaration and capability constraints.
Motorcycle/Car: licence + vehicle details/evidence according to approved production verification policy .
Safety/privacy
Courier should receive only the data required for the current delivery . Cross-campus/transfer capability is separate from
ordinary local courier capability .
