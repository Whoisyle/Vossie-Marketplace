Developer Series 2 — Figma and motion production
Series 2 made Figma the UI/UX source of truth and defined:
• variables/tokens,
• primitives,
• reusable components,
• role-specific patterns,
• responsive states,
• full 177-screen assembly ,
• Figma MCP as an accelerator where access permits,
• Motion.dev/CSS as the runtime motion layer,
• Motion.so as optional exported-media generation only .
The rule was to build a design system first, not generate 177 disconnected mockups.
