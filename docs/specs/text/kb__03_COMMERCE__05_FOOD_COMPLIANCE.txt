Product approach
F ood listings should require stronger structured information and traceability:
• ingredients,
• allergens,
• prep/availability time,
• seller identity ,
• food-specific reviews,
• order/batch/preparation timestamps where useful,
• clear report-food-safety flow.
Seller responsibility
V ossie provides platform rules, guidance, traceability and incident handling. Sellers remain responsible for meeting appli-
cable food laws and campus requirements.
Incident flow
F ood safety report → evidence/order linkage → urgent Operations review → affected listing/batch/seller may be paused
→ trace related orders → escalate according to policy .
Production dependency
Actual compliance requirements must be validated with Eduvos and relevant municipal/environmental-health authorities.
