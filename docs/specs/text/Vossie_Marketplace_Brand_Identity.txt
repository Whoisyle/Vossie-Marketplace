--- slide 3
03
The design direction
How it works
Uber-inspired task flows: location first, clear choices, dispatch requests, live status and one obvious next action.
How it feels
Plato-inspired restraint: warm ivory, soft blue, rounded lettering, quiet packaging and generous space.
Vossie brings these references together in an original campus marketplace identity.

--- slide 9
09
Logo rules
Space and scale
Leave at least one “o” height of clear space around the full wordmark. Use the icon when the descriptor becomes too small to read.
Consistent artwork
Keep the joined letters intact. Avoid stretching, extra effects, busy backgrounds or new monograms.
Proposed starting sizes: full lockup 180 px digital / 35 mm print. Icon 32 px. Review proofs at actual size.

--- slide 12
12
Packaging details
The visual system
Ivory surfaces, navy wordmark and one powder-blue panel. A small seal carries the joined “ss”.
The practical layer
Choose packaging by item size and protection needs. Keep order labels separate from brand artwork.
Production brief
Supplier dielines, print proofs, material selection and closure tests follow this concept. Food items need suitable food-contact packaging. Do not print unnecessary student details.
