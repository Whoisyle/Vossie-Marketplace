Seller Studio
The deeper seller business-management experience includes:
• store profile,
• listing creation/editing,
• media,
• inventory ,
• pricing/promotions,
• orders,
• earnings,
• payouts,
• seller analytics,
• timetable/availability ,
• Reels/content analytics.
Timetable idea
Student uploads a timetable PDF → prototype extracts class times → student must review/confirm → availability is
generated → student can manually block extra time even after class.
Do not treat extracted timetable data as authoritative without user confirmation. A void retaining the source PDF longer
than needed where possible.
Incubation
Incubation is not simply seller admin. It sees aggregate/business-support intelligence:
• unmet searches,
• high-demand categories,
• stock-outs,
• seller growth,
• repeat demand,
• campus opportunities,
• business-health indicators,
• interventions and 30-day plans.
The distinction: seller sees their business; Incubation sees opportunity/support intelligence across authorized campus scope.
