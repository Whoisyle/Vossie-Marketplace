Marketplace Reels
Marketplace Reels is a shoppable short-video layer tied to real commerce objects.
Buyer experience
• first-class Reels tab in Marketplace navigation,
• vertical swipe feed,
• seller handle/profile,
• short caption,
• linked product/service/rental/digital item,
• pinned product rail,
• multiple linked listings where appropriate,
• tap through to product/store/cart without leaving commerce context.
Seller experience
Create Reel → record/upload → trim to roughly 15–30 seconds → caption → link listing(s) → preview → publish.
Seller analytics should include views, product taps, add-to-cart, bookings/orders and conversion signals.
Boundaries
• no requirement for open comments in the pilot,
• content must remain commerce-led,
• moderation/reporting required,
• a Reel must link to legitimate marketplace inventory/service content.
