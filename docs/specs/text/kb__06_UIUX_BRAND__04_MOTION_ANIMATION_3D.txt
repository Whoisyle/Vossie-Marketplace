Motion, Animation and Optional 3D
Principle
F unctional motion first. Optional 3D second. Motion explains cause/effect and state.
Three layers
1. Presses, forms, navigation and recovery .
2. Matching, status, tracking and scanning.
3. Optional parcel preview/icon depth/campus illustration.
Core experiences must work without layer 3.
Existing motion inventory

• logo reveal,
• account-step transitions,
• button/submit feedback,
• main navigation,
• workspace switch,
• search continuity ,
• sheets/filters,
• product-detail continuity ,
• basket/quantity ,
• seller matching,
• request arrival/acceptance,
• money-state transition,
• order milestones,
• courier interpolation,
• scan recognition,
• handover,
• manifest reconciliation,
• availability/workload,
• listing publication,
• messaging/notifications,
• incubation panels/plans,
• operations queue updates,
• loading/offline/recovery .
Runtime
Core motion uses CSS/Motion.dev-style implementation. Motion.so or other generative motion is optional exported media
only and cannot be a runtime dependency for a zero-budget prototype.
Accessibility
Respect reduced motion. Never make animation the only carrier of meaning.
