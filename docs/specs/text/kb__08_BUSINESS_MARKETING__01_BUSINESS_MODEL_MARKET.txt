Business thesis
V ossie creates value across demand, supply , fulfilment and institutional support.
• Buyers: relevant campus supply , trust context, fulfilment visibility .
• Sellers: verified demand, storefront, orders, analytics, content discovery .
• Couriers: local earning opportunities with structured jobs.
• Eduvos/Incubation: visibility into entrepreneurship and unmet demand.
Monetization order
Do not optimize monetization before density and trust.
