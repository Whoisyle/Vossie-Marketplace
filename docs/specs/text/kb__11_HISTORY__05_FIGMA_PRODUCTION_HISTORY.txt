Brand source of truth
The approved Brand Identity controls logo, palette, typography , spacing and visual language.
The design language is soft and restrained, inspired by Plato’s visual literacy but not copied. Interaction behaviour is
informed by Uber-style task clarity , live status, maps, contextual cards and sheets.
