Recovery Limits and Archive Integrity
What this archive contains
This archive contains the complete recoverable V ossie project knowledge available from:
• current conversation context,
• recovered prior-conversation context,
• saved project artifacts,
• platform strategy revisions,
• UI/UX files,
• developer series,
• business/marketing documents,
• and the latest confirmed decisions.
Important distinction
This knowledge base is intended to transfer the project accurately to a fresh account. It is not a guaranteed verbatim export
of every historical ChatGPT message ever sent across every separate chat, because chat transcripts that are not present in
current context or saved as artifacts cannot be reconstructed word-for-word without inventing text.
Where exact old wording was unavailable, the archive preserves the decision, rationale, workflow and artifact that resulted
from it.
What must never be invented during migration
• Eduvos institutional approval.
• Live Microsoft/Entra integration access.
• Live payment credentials.
• Government identity verification access.
• Live logistics-provider contracts.
• Production readiness.
• F eatures not present in the approved product decisions.
