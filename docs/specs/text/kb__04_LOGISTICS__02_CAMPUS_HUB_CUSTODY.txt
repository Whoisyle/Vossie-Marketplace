Campus Hub and Package Custody
Campus Hub is the physical custody point inside the V ossie operating model.
Core workflows
• incoming package queue,
• scan/identify package,
• condition check,
• accept/reject/exception,
• custody state,
• ready for collection,
• courier handoff,
• outgoing transfer,
• returns.
QR
Package identifiers/QRs are generated by the backend/system and displayed/printed by the appropriate workflow. Scanning
never directly grants a final state without server-side validation.
Custody chain
Every handoff records who/what role, where, timestamp, package/order and state transition.
Example:
Seller → Origin Hub → Local Courier/T ransfer → Destination Hub → Courier/Buyer.
Disputes
Custody scans, condition records, OTP delivery and timestamps become evidence for Operations.
