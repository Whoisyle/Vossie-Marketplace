Cinematic pitch structure
The pitch was deliberately split into two modes.
Act I — cinematic introduction
The first approximately four minutes introduce the problem, V ossie concept and team contributions without fully demon-
strating the application.
Opening concept:
1. Black screen.
2. A single V ossie dot appears.
3. Dot bounces with controlled impact/audio rhythm.
4. Final landing becomes the dot above the i in the V ossie wordmark.
5. Camera pushes through the dot into an illustrated campus world.
6. F ragmented student needs/supply are visualized.
7. T eam contributions transform the problem into product, design, engineering and coordinated execution.
